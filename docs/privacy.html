Privacy Policy

Laggy World · Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Clipper handles. Clipper is the publishing tool that Laggy World uses to post videos, photos and text to its own social media accounts. It covers the Clipper app registered with Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google (YouTube) and TikTok.

What Clipper is

Clipper runs privately on Laggy World's own computer. It is not a public service: people can't sign up or log in to it, and it has no users other than the Laggy World team. Clipper only publishes to accounts that Laggy World owns or manages and has connected itself.

Information we handle

  • Access tokens for our own accounts. When we connect our Facebook Page, Instagram account, YouTube channel or TikTok account, the platform gives Clipper an access token so it can publish on our behalf.
  • Basic account details. The name, ID and profile picture of the accounts we connect, so we can tell them apart.
  • Our own content and its performance. The videos, images and captions we publish, and the view and engagement counts of our own posts, so we can see what works.
  • Public video information. To find the best moments in a video, Clipper may read publicly available YouTube data for that video, such as captions, the most-replayed graph, live chat timing and timestamps mentioned in public comments. It uses this only to measure which moments are popular and does not build profiles of the people who wrote them.

Clipper does not collect information about people who visit, follow or interact with our pages. It doesn't read private messages, use tracking cookies or analytics, or collect payment information.

How we use it

  • To publish and schedule our own posts, Reels and videos on the platforms above.
  • To show us the status and view counts of what we've published.
  • To choose clip moments and write titles, captions and hashtags for our content.

We don't sell, rent or trade any information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Sharing

Information is shared only as needed to do the work above:

  • Meta, Google (YouTube) and TikTok receive the content we publish to them through their official APIs, under their own privacy policies.
  • An AI service (an OpenAI-compatible provider such as DeepSeek) may receive video transcripts, titles and topic descriptions to suggest clip moments and write captions. We don't send access tokens or personal information about other people.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Storage and security

Everything Clipper stores stays on Laggy World's own computer, not on a hosted server. Access tokens and API keys are encrypted. The app is reachable only from that computer. We keep information only while we use the connected accounts, and we delete it when we disconnect them.

Data deletion

Clipper only holds data for accounts Laggy World owns. To have data removed:

  • Facebook / Instagram: remove the app in Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites). Its access stops at once and the stored token no longer works. We remove the account from Clipper when we disconnect it.
  • YouTube: remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok: remove the app in TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Anything else: message us (see below), and we'll delete the data you ask about within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Children

Clipper isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly handle children's personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
