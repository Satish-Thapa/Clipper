Cheat

Paste reel links from Facebook, Instagram or YouTube. Each one is downloaded at the best quality there is (up to 4K), made into a 4K copy as it is (no cutting, no text added), and put in a free slot. The daily limit still applies.

Up to 3 min = a short (short slots), longer = a long (up to 15 min). The title is the reel's own, with a credit line for its creator: edit it in Clips. Instagram may need you to be logged in (cookies); if a link fails, its reason shows below. Reposting other people's videos can get copyright claims on your accounts.

Reels from your messages

Send a reel from your main account to your Facebook Page in Messenger, or to your Instagram account in a DM: within a minute it shows up below and downloads by itself (4K, into a slot).
