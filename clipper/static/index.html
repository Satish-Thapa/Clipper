Autopilot

Tell it how many clips to post a day and when. Every day it finds what's trending (this week's most-viewed YouTube videos, read by DeepSeek), clips the best videos and posts them spread over your posting hours. Changes apply right away, even while it's working.

When one goes live, the autopilot pauses (its round and topics stop, scheduled posts are held) and the stream is clipped live as streamer + gameplay, posting to the platforms above. When the stream ends, the held posts go back on schedule and the autopilot carries on.

News & facts posts

Give a topic (e.g. "GTA 5"), or leave it empty to use the autopilot's niche / what your views say works / what's trending. It searches news portals (Google News, Bing News), reads the articles, and DeepSeek writes a short post from their facts only, credited to the source. Each post is a 7-15s vertical video (picture, headline, key facts, "Source: ...") that you post now, at a set time, or on the autopilot's slots.

Settings

What's stored, and a clean start. Platform keys live in .env; autopilot settings are on the Auto page.

🤖 AI models

Every AI call (clip picks, titles, voice-over, autopilot, news) goes to the Main AI. If it's down, errors or gives a broken answer, the next one gets the same request. The logs say which one answered. AIs without a key in .env are skipped.

Background videos (split layout)

For Framing → Split: clip on top + your video below: the follow message and headline sit on top, then the clip whole (16:9, nothing cut off), and one of these plays muted below it (from a random point, looped if it's short), with captions at the bottom and the same end card. Any video works (gameplay, satisfying clips, parkour…); it's cropped to fill the half.
